Ride to Market on Main and skip the parking hassle.

I was there from 5 till the end, met a lot of great people, and had energizing conversations about the future of Heber.

It was awesome to see so many arrive by bike, e-bike, and e-scooter.

Huge thanks to the Wasatch Trail Foundation and the Trek store—Mia and Tori have been running this bike check every Thursday all summer.

I handed out more seed packets while helping watch over everyone's bikes.
